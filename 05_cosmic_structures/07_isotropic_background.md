## **5.7. The Structural Self-Closure of Cosmic Background Interpretation**

The cosmic microwave background serves simultaneously as the primary empirical anchor of $\Lambda$CDM and as the object from which its parameters are derived. Its thermal spectrum is read as evidence of primordial plasma; its anisotropy spectrum as evidence of inflationary fluctuations; its dipole as evidence of local kinematic motion. Each inference is individually defensible. Taken together, they exhibit a structural pattern: each interpretive move presupposes an element of the framework that the broader interpretation is meant to establish.

The chapter identifies three independent self-closures in the conventional CMB interpretation, locates them relative to existing critiques that circle around them without naming them, documents the failure of standard defenses against the resulting coupling claim, and concludes by outlining the projection reading that dissolves them.

**Isotropy as a Property of Decomposition**

The claim that the CMB is isotropic requires a prior definition of what would count as anisotropic. The definition is operational, not neutral.

* **Decomposition basis:** spherical harmonics on a sphere at the last-scattering distance, in a comoving frame chosen to minimize the dipole.
* **Anisotropy ascription:** deviations quantified relative to this basis.
* **Constitutive assumption:** the basis is constructed to be isotropic by fiat.
* **Foreclosed question:** whether the underlying differentiation structure is isotropic in any stronger sense cannot be answered from within the chosen representation.

The observed residual $\Delta T / T \approx 10^{-5}$ makes the circularity operational rather than merely philosophical. The standard account reads the CMB as "nearly isotropic with statistically isotropic perturbations." Under examination:

* **Statistical isotropy** is a property of the generating ensemble, not of any single realization.
* **Ergodicity** is invoked to equate the observed realization with the ensemble average.
* **Independent testing** of the ensemble assumption from the same data set is not available. The assumption is input, not result.

**First self-closure:** identification of the CMB as isotropic presupposes a symmetric decomposition that is constructed to detect what it is supposed to verify.

**Known Critiques Within the State Ontology**

The circularity has been approached from several directions over half a century. Each critique identifies part of the structural problem without naming it as a closure.

**Penrose — Weyl Curvature Hypothesis (1979 ff.).**

* **Claim:** the early universe's low gravitational entropy, expressed as vanishing Weyl curvature at the initial hypersurface, is a condition of extraordinary improbability.
* **Quantification:** phase-space estimate of roughly $1$ part in $10^{10^{123}}$.
* **Consequence drawn:** no process compatible with standard physics, including inflation, generates such a state without requiring an equally improbable antecedent.
* **Proposed resolution:** Conformal Cyclic Cosmology, embedding the initial state in a larger cyclic framework.
* **What is not asked:** whether the phase space is the right analytical object — whether enumerating alternative initial states presupposes a decomposition whose validity is independent of the object.

**Steinhardt, Ijjas, and Loeb — Initial Patch Problem (2017 ff.).**

* **Claim:** inflation does not generically produce a homogeneous, isotropic universe. Measure-theoretically, inflationary initial conditions yield a landscape in which homogeneous outcomes form a negligible subset.
* **Consequence drawn:** inflation requires a sufficiently smooth initial patch as input. The homogeneity it was introduced to explain is presupposed.
* **Proposed resolution:** ekpyrotic and cyclic alternatives that generate smoothness from generic initial conditions.
* **What is not asked:** whether homogeneity admits mechanistic explanation at all, or whether "homogeneous" is a property of a decomposition rather than of a state.

**Albert, Price, Carroll — Past Hypothesis.**

* **Claim:** the observed time-asymmetry of entropy requires that the universe began in a state of extraordinarily low entropy, imposed as a boundary condition not derivable from dynamics.
* **Carroll's formulation:** "the greatest unexplained fact in physics."
* **What is not asked:** whether "the entropy of the initial state" is well-defined independently of the observational categories used to quantify it.

**Shared pattern.** All three critiques accept the existence of an initial state, characterize it by an extensive property (Weyl curvature, smoothness, entropy), and seek mechanisms or boundary conditions to account for why that state obtained. None questions whether the categorical apparatus that ascribes such properties to an initial state is itself well-posed. Under the self-closure framing, the improbability of low-Weyl initial states, the non-genericity of inflationary smoothing, and the thermodynamic demand for a low-entropy boundary are symptoms of a single methodological feature: a framework that requires a symmetric or smooth initial state to function, and that cannot independently justify the decomposition through which "symmetric" and "smooth" are defined.

**Expansion and the Spectral Purity Problem**

A second self-closure arises in the relationship between the CMB spectrum and admissible expansion regimes.

**Empirical bound — FIRAS on COBE (Fixsen et al. 1996; Fixsen 2009).**

* Compton $y$-parameter: $|y| < 1.5 \times 10^{-5}$
* Chemical potential $\mu$: $|\mu| < 9 \times 10^{-5}$
* Precision exceeding any laboratory blackbody calibration by orders of magnitude.

**Subluminal expansion regimes.**

* Different plasma elements at decoupling observed with different Doppler factors.
* Superposition of blackbodies at different temperatures is not a blackbody.
* Expected signature: $y$-type spectral distortion.
* FIRAS excludes this signature.

**Superluminal (inflationary) regimes.**

* Metric expansion removes relative motion from the emission geometry; no differential Doppler.
* Primordial $l = 1$ mode exits the Hubble horizon, freezes, re-enters as coherent large-scale structure.
* The mode is not diluted by linear evolution.
* Expected signature: observable primordial dipole, inconsistent with the isotropy hypothesis.

**Dilemma.**

* Subluminal $\rightarrow$ fails spectral purity.
* Superluminal $\rightarrow$ fails isotropy.
* Standard model occupies a single permitted point: metric expansion with Bunch-Davies vacuum on a quasi-de-Sitter background, with the $l = 1$ mode set to zero by vacuum symmetry.

**Second self-closure:** the expansion regime is not derived from first principles. It is selected by requiring compatibility with the observed spectral purity and isotropy. The observed properties select the regime that is then presented as explaining them.

**The Dipole and the Decomposition Convention**

The observed CMB dipole has amplitude $\Delta T / T \approx 1.23 \times 10^{-3}$, two orders of magnitude above higher multipoles. Its interpretation is conventional rather than measured.

* **Convention:** the frame in which the dipole vanishes is *defined* as the CMB rest frame. The boost required to reach it is *defined* as the observer's peculiar velocity.
* **Derived kinematic velocity:** approximately $370$ km/s toward Leo.
* **Independent validation** of the CMB rest frame would require agreement with frames derived from distant matter distributions. It does not obtain.

**Ellis-Baldwin test (Ellis & Baldwin 1984).**

* Prediction under pure kinematic interpretation: matter-distribution dipoles agree in amplitude and direction with the CMB dipole, modulo a known spectral factor.
* Secrest et al. (2021, 2022), WISE quasars: matter dipole $\sim 2\times$ the kinematic prediction, $4.9\sigma$ tension.
* Singal (2011); Rameez et al. (2018), radio galaxy catalogs: consistent discrepancies.

**Bulk flow measurements.**

* Kashlinsky et al. (2008, 2010): coherent cluster motions across hundreds of Mpc exceeding $\Lambda$CDM predictions.
* Watkins, Feldman & Hudson (2009) and subsequent analyses: persistent excess, variously confirmed and disputed.
* Watkins et al. (2023), Cosmicflows-4: bulk flow on $200 \, h^{-1}$ Mpc at $\sim 4.8\sigma$ tension with $\Lambda$CDM.

**Low-multipole directional anomalies.**

* Axis of evil — quadrupole–octupole alignment (Land & Magueijo 2005; Copi et al. 2010).
* Hemispherical power asymmetry (Eriksen et al. 2004; Planck Collaboration 2014, 2020).
* Cold Spot (Vielva et al. 2004).
* Each individually marginal. Directions not obviously uncorrelated with each other or with the dipole axis.

**Causal-structural argument.**

* If the CMB anisotropy field is the seed of all subsequent structure formation, peculiar velocities at any later epoch are deterministic functionals of the primordial anisotropy field plus gravitational dynamics.
* No causally independent source of bulk motion exists in linear or quasi-linear perturbation theory.
* Earth's motion, bulk flow, and low-multipole directional anomalies are therefore not independent phenomena set alongside CMB anisotropies. They are different projections of a single underlying structure.

**Third self-closure:** the decomposition separating kinematic dipole from intrinsic anisotropy is ill-defined when the CMB is causally upstream of the kinematics. Each downstream test receives as its null the assumption whose tension with the data is the object of investigation.

**Defenses of Separability**

Six defenses of the standard decomposition exist. None restores separability.

* **Upstream quantum randomness.**

* *Defense:* primordial perturbations are random draws from Bunch-Davies; no determinate structure.
* *Failure:* once the draw is fixed — which for any observed universe is necessarily the case — anisotropy and velocity fields are related by deterministic dynamics. Randomness locates the specific values, not the coupling.

* **Statistical isotropy of the ensemble.**

* *Defense:* individual realizations may have directional structure; the generating ensemble is isotropic.
* *Failure:* circular. Ensemble isotropy is the assumption under investigation.

* **Horizon exclusion of the primordial dipole.**

* *Defense:* the $l = 1$ mode is stretched outside the Hubble horizon during inflation. The observed dipole must therefore be kinematic.
* *Failure:* self-undermining. If the primordial dipole is causally disconnected from the observable patch, kinematic attribution becomes a definition rather than a result. The Ellis-Baldwin tension then has no remaining explanation under the same assumption.

* **Nonlinear scale separation.**

* *Defense:* local peculiar velocities are dominated by late-time nonlinear attractors (Great Attractor, Shapley Concentration), not primordial modes.
* *Failure:* quantitative only. Late-time structure is deterministic evolution of the primordial field. Bulk-flow measurements at $100$–$300 \, h^{-1}$ Mpc sit in the linear regime where the quantitative weakening does not apply.

* **Late-time integrated Sachs-Wolfe.**

* *Defense:* part of the observed anisotropy is not primordial but generated by photon transit through evolving potentials.
* *Failure:* inverts the coupling rather than removing it. CMB and late-time structure become bidirectionally coupled; separability worsens.

* **Decoherence and the quantum-classical transition.**

* *Defense:* the anisotropy field acquires determinate values only through decoherence; no ontological field exists to couple to kinematics prior to this transition.
* *Failure:* reinforces the projection reading. If the field is context-dependent, the decomposition into CMB and peculiar velocity is an artifact of the decomposition procedure, not a factoring of an independently existing state.

**Summary.** Three defenses (1, 4, 5) leave the coupling intact or weaken it only quantitatively. Two (2, 3) are circular or self-undermining. One (6) strengthens the projection reading. No defense of the standard decomposition successfully restores separability. Any further proposed mechanism of decoupling must explain why Ellis-Baldwin, Cosmicflows-4, and the low-multipole alignments are not manifestations of precisely the coupling it would have to break.

**The Projection Question**

The three self-closures converge on a single structural feature: each inference builds its answer into the decomposition. This is not ordinary theory-ladenness. It is a systematic property of treating the CMB as a *state* — a configuration at a time whose properties admit direct empirical characterization.

The alternative framing treats the CMB as a *projection*:

* **Object:** a two-dimensional section through a differentiation structure, taken at the scale where photon–matter coupling transitions into transparency from the observer's position.
* **Observables:** properties of the projected section, not necessarily of any underlying state.
* **Grammar shift:**
  * "The CMB is isotropic" $\rightarrow$ "the projection exhibits near-uniformity under the chosen decomposition."
  * "The dipole is due to our peculiar motion" $\rightarrow$ "the dipole is the directional component of the projection removable by a boost in the decomposition."
  * "The bulk flow is an anomaly" $\rightarrow$ "the bulk flow is a different projection of the same directional asymmetry the dipole expresses."

No numerical prediction changes. The self-closures dissolve:

* The decomposition is acknowledged as constitutive of observational categories, not read as a passive instrument.
* The symmetric limit is a property of the decomposition, not of a hypothetical underlying state.
* The coupling between dipole, peculiar velocity, bulk flow, and directional anomalies is structural, not anomalous.

**What the Data Already Show**

The accumulated observational tensions share a common feature: each is a directional or coupling tension between quantities that the standard decomposition treats as separable but that structure-formation physics implies are not.

* **Ellis-Baldwin discrepancy:** $\sim 4.9\sigma$
* **Cosmicflows-4 bulk flow:** $\sim 4.8\sigma$
* **Low-multipole directional alignments:** persistent across Planck data releases
* **Hemispherical asymmetry:** persistent across Planck data releases

**Statistical significance under the separability assumption.**

* Each anomaly is evaluated against a null generated from the assumption that dipole, primordial anisotropy, and late-time structure separate cleanly.
* The reported $\sigma$-values therefore measure tension between data and the separability assumption itself, not between data and underlying physics.
* Because the separability assumption is simultaneously the assumption that would have to fail for the anomalies to be structurally coupled, their joint significance does not compose additively in the standard sense.

**Distinctive prediction of the projection reading.** If dipole, bulk flow, and low-multipole directional anomalies are projections of a single directional asymmetry, their directions and amplitudes should exhibit correlations beyond the null predictions of separability. Partial evidence exists:

* Dipole direction and certain low-multipole alignments are approximately coincident.
* Bulk flow directions from independent surveys cluster non-randomly with respect to both.

**Next empirical step.** Joint analysis of dipole, bulk flow, and low-multipole directions as projections of a common structural asymmetry, rather than as independent observables whose correlations require separate explanation. This is not a test of a new theoretical framework. It is a reanalysis of existing data under a framing the current methodology does not accommodate.

**Synthesis**

Three self-closures structure the standard CMB interpretation:

* **Isotropy** ascribed relative to a decomposition constructed to detect it.
* **Expansion regime** selected by requiring compatibility with observed spectral purity and isotropy.
* **Kinematic decomposition of the dipole** ill-defined when the CMB is causally upstream of the kinematics.

The critiques of Penrose, Steinhardt, and the Past Hypothesis identify components of the problem but remain within the state ontology that generates it. The six standard defenses of separability either leave the coupling intact, are circular, or reinforce the projection reading.

The projection framing dissolves the closures without introducing new physics, by treating the observed sky as a section through a larger structure rather than as a snapshot of a configuration. The empirical program that follows is modest: joint directional analyses of accumulated tensions, and reassessment of spectral distortion bounds as constraints on admissible projection geometries rather than on expansion history. Whether this framing supports one interpretive framework or another is a question that can be deferred. The prior question — whether the standard interpretation performs inferential work its methodology cannot independently underwrite — can be answered from the existing data.
