## **8.7. Dependency Propagation as the Unified Principle**

This chapter develops the reading in which the fundamental physical quantities — time, mass, space, and gravitation — are treated not as ontologically distinct substrates but as projective aspects of a single underlying process: dependency propagation through differentiation structure. On this reading the speed of light $c$ is interpreted not primarily as a velocity limit but as the irreducible rate of dependency update, with the familiar physical phenomena following as structural consequences. Relativistic mechanics, quantum mechanics, and gravitation are then recast as scale-dependent projections of the same dependency architecture, consistent with their standard descriptions in their respective domains. Quantum mechanical phenomena — superposition, uncertainty, entanglement, decoherence — are offered a reinterpretation as manifestations of incomplete or distributed update processes within dependency networks (interpretation).

**The Elimination of Computational Ontology**

Classical and computational approaches to physics implicitly presuppose an agent — whether physical law, mathematical structure, or computational process — that performs operations upon states. This framing introduces an ontological dualism between the operator and the operated-upon, between the rule and its application. ISE eliminates this dualism by recognizing that there exists no computational substrate executing transformations. What appears as computation or operation is itself a projection effect arising from the network of potential vectors standing in mutual dependency.

When information undergoes acceleration, the dependency graph updates through pattern propagation. No entity calculates; the structure simply is its own transformation. Arranging all changes into a relative sequence defines time itself — time emerges through sequence length, through state changes. A quantity of information simultaneously constitutes a sequence length of differentiation, and therefore time. The analogy to computation is merely that: an analogy useful for initial comprehension but ultimately dissolved by the theory's own implications.

**The Dependency Structure Ontology**

The foundational claim is stark: there exists only dependency structure. When node A changes, node B changes, node C changes — this chain of dependencies constitutes the totality of physical reality. From this minimal ontology, the classical physical quantities emerge not as fundamental categories but as projective aspects of the same underlying architecture.

**Time as Seriality**

Time is read here not as a container within which sequences unfold but as the seriality of dependency itself — the fact that sequences exist at all. This reading complements the Newtonian picture of absolute time as a backdrop and the relativistic picture of time as a dimension of the spacetime manifold by locating temporal duration in the serialization of dependency chains: time, on this account, has no existence independent of those chains, and chain length corresponds to the duration experienced at that structural level (interpretation).

**Mass as Dependency Depth**

Mass is not a property that an entity possesses. Mass is how much dependency a pattern contains. Mass is sequence depth. This reconceptualization explains inertia as the update overhead required for restructuring complex dependency networks and explains gravitational coupling as proportional interaction between dependency densities. A massive object is not a thing with the property of mass but a region of dependency structure with characteristic depth.

**Space as Relational Structure**

The spatial manifold emerges as the relational structure of the vector network — the pattern of connections between dependency nodes projected onto an apparent three-dimensional (or higher-dimensional) configuration. Spatial separation reflects dependency distance: the number of intermediary updates required for influence propagation between structures. Curvature emerges from variations in dependency density, consistent with general relativistic descriptions while grounding them in more fundamental relational architecture.

**The Reductive Sequence**

The complete reductive sequence from protoinformation to observable physics proceeds as follows. Protoinformation constitutes pure differentiation capacity — the potential for distinction without yet actualized distinction. Differentiation generates dependency — the actualization of distinction creates relational bonds. Dependency generates seriality (time) — ordered relations produce sequential structure. Seriality generates sequence length (mass) — the depth of sequential structure manifests as inertial and gravitational properties. Sequence length generates inertia and gravitation — the observable mechanical and gravitational phenomena emerge from structural depth. Each level is the same fundamental process viewed from a different projective angle.

**The Photon as Limiting Case**

The photon serves here as the limiting case with minimal dependency depth — effectively a single node. On that reading any change propagates immediately; there is no sequence of internal updates, hence no internal time and no rest mass. The photon marks the boundary at which seriality begins: below this threshold — at dependency depths less than one — no dependency chain is present, no internal time is defined, and propagation occurs at $c$ (interpretation).

This explains why c is not merely a velocity but the fundamental rate constant of reality: c represents one update per one unit of base time, which is itself defined by the minimal update operation. The speed of light is the indivisible quantum of dependency propagation.

**Gravitation as Universal c-Rate Interaction**

Gravitational influence propagates at $c$ — a well-established result in general relativity. Read through dependency propagation, this follows directly from the stipulation that all dependency updates propagate at the base rate: the effect on every individual node occurs at $c$, and no slower propagation channel is introduced (interpretation).

However, structures with different dependency depths respond differently to gravitational updates. A photon, with its single-node structure, receives one update and completes its response — it propagates at c. A proton, with n nodes, requires n updates that must achieve coherence — during this process, a probability distribution over space emerges. The deeper the dependency structure, the longer the coherent update requires, and the more pronounced the apparent deviation from c-propagation.

**Quantum Mechanics as Update Distribution**

This framework provides a natural reinterpretation of quantum mechanical phenomena without invoking observer-dependent collapse or hidden variables.

**The Wave Function as Update State**

Consider a massive particle such as a proton. Where is it located? Read through dependency propagation, this question becomes: what is the current state of the update process across its node network? The wave function is then interpreted as the probability distribution over the update state of the dependency network — not as Copenhagen-style fundamental indeterminacy but as a reflection that the update process is not yet complete (interpretation).

**Superposition as Parallel Update Paths**

Superposition states arise when multiple update paths through the dependency network remain simultaneously viable. The system has not yet resolved which path the coherent update will follow. This is not a statement about our knowledge but about the objective state of the dependency structure.

**Measurement as Update Completion**

Measurement corresponds to the moment when sufficient nodes achieve coherent update such that a definite position becomes projectable at the observer's scale. Wave function collapse is update completion. The measurement apparatus, itself a dependency structure, couples with the measured system and forces resolution of the update path through its own coherence requirements.

**The Apparatus as Dependency Structure**

Critically, the measurement apparatus is not a "classical" device external to quantum mechanics but itself a dependency network with its own update dynamics. Whether the probe is a photon, electron beam, or macroscopic detector, it possesses:

* Dependency depth (mass/inertia from its node network)
* Continuous update dynamics across its structure
* Resonance coupling requirements for interaction

When probe and target couple, two dependency networks engage in continuous resonance interaction. Throughout this coupling, both structures maintain complete differential specification — each node influences neighboring nodes according to dependency propagation rules at rate c. This continuous interaction contains full trajectory information encoded in the update dynamics.

**The Heisenberg uncertainty constrains the projection of this continuous update interaction onto discrete classical eigenvalues**, not the interaction itself. The statistical distribution (wave function) describes viable update paths; measurement selects a path by forcing coherence through the apparatus's own dependency requirements. Copenhagen's error was ontologizing this projection limitation as fundamental indeterminacy while treating the apparatus as categorically distinct from the quantum system.

**Entanglement as Coupled Updates**

Entangled particles are coupled nodes that update together. Their correlations do not require signal propagation because they share dependency structure — updates to one are updates to both, not because information travels between them but because they constitute a single extended dependency pattern. Bell inequality violations follow naturally: the correlations are structural, not communicative.

**Decoherence as Environmental Path Selection**

Environmental decoherence occurs when the surrounding dependency structure — the environment — couples with the system and forces update path decisions. The environment, with its vast dependency complexity, overwhelms the system's internal update options and selects definite outcomes. This explains why macroscopic objects do not exhibit superposition: their coupling to environmental dependency structures constantly forces path resolution.

**The Nature of Photon Wavelength**

A photon has no intrinsic spatial extension because it possesses no internal structure — no interior space within which extent could be defined. It constitutes a single node, a minimal differentiation. What then is the wavelength that characterizes electromagnetic radiation?

The wavelength is not the size of the photon but the characteristic coupling distance in the dependency network — how the photon couples with the network structure. The wave is not the photon; the wave is the probability distribution of its interaction with the dependency architecture. We never measure a photon with length x; we measure detection events whose statistical distribution yields wavelength.

Frequency, correspondingly, is not oscillations per unit time but coupling strength with the network. Higher frequency indicates denser coupling, more energy, shorter characteristic distance. The photon does not oscillate; it propagates with a coupling signature. This dissolves the wave-particle duality: the particle is the dependency node, the wave is its interaction distribution.

**The Unified Description**

The entire framework reduces to a single statement: physical reality consists of dependency propagation through differentiation structure with c as the base rate.

The fundamental relations are: Mass is proportional to dependency depth (node count). Time is proportional to sequence length (update steps). Space is proportional to relational structure (the vector network). Gravitation is proportional to operation density (updates per spatial volume). c equals one update per one base time unit — indivisible.

Read consistently, the account connects special and general relativity ($c$ as limit because it is the indivisible base operation), quantum mechanics (probability distributions as update-state distributions), gravitation and mass-energy equivalence (both as dependency structure), and inertia (as update overhead) within a single vocabulary. It also suggests, speculatively, reinterpretations of open problems: dark matter as a scale effect, dark energy as differentiation expansion, singularities as scale transitions rather than physical infinities, the measurement problem as update completion, and the unification question as a matter of locating a shared substrate rather than joining separate theories. Each of these remains a proposal to be formalized and tested (speculation).

**Existence as Sequence**

To exist means to stand in dependency relations. To exist more means to stand in more dependency relations — this we call mass. The differences between relativistic, quantum, and gravitational physics dissolve as projection effects of structural depth. A network of dependency relations with propagation rules at c and structural depth as mass is computable, simulable, and testable. The mathematical formalization awaits, but the conceptual structure is complete: all of physics viewed as dependency propagation.
