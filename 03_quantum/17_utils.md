## **3.17. Ultimate Utilitarianism**

This chapter outlines a time-and-causality program within a theory-of-everything (TOE) reading that treats substances, media, and pre-given spacetime as derived rather than primitive. Relational gradient patterns—“wobbles”—are taken as the sole primitives of this sketch. Wobbles are non-substantial, infinitely fine-grained structures whose only “being” is relational. Observation is modeled as the interference of a “gradient wobble” with an “observer wobble,” neither of which exists as a thing, nor in a medium such as space or time. What exists is a projection: an act of individuation that yields a combined interference pattern. This pattern extends in a strict relation to its seed configuration, generating what appears as a causal sequence. The apparent temporal order and spatial separation are emergent artifacts of how projections differentiate and align relational structures across scales. There is no ontological “now,” only an abstract equivalence relation over congruent patterns; the interference structure “continues” without a medium of continuation, and “simultaneity” merely names the absence of an imposed ordering. Time and space are not entities; they are the organized shadows cast by scale-dependent projection on an underlying, non-substantial relational manifold.

**Core Structure Without Substance**

- No ontic “things”: neither matter nor fields exist as substances.
- No pre-existing spacetime container: there is no medium in which events occur.
- Only relational gradient patterns (“wobbles”) exist, with unbounded fractal resolution.

**Causality as Interference-Linearity**

Interference → Combined Pattern → Continuation constrained by its seed

1. A gradient wobble couples with an observer wobble via interference.
2. The superposition yields a new pattern (a composite wobble).
3. The continuation of that pattern remains in a strict relation to its generating configuration.

This structured continuation is what we call causality. It is not a force or an ontic law, but the geometric necessity of relational continuity under a given projection. Crucially, “linearity” here is not an ontic property of reality but an artifact of how a projection organizes interference outcomes so that they appear linear.

**Emergence of Time**

Time is not:
- A fundamental dimension or parameter,
- A flow or substance,
- An intrinsic property of the world.

Time is the projective appearance generated by ordered interference relations:
- A chain of interference-induced reorganizations provides apparent “succession.”
- Apparent delays arise as effective reconfiguration latencies within and across relational chains.
- This induces an operational “runtime” between chains that functions as spatiality, while the ordering along a chain functions as temporality.

Formally, if $w_{k+1} = \mathcal{I}(w_k, \tilde{w}_k)$ for a suitable interference map $\mathcal{I}$, a projection $P$ induces a sequence $x_{k+1} = F(x_k)$ with $x_k = P(w_k)$ that is read as temporal evolution.

**Emergence of Space**

Space arises from the relative ordering among multiple causal chains:
- Distinct interference patterns extend “in parallel.”
- Their differing projective continuations generate relative displacements.
- The structure of these displacements manifests as emergent spatial separation.

Space is thus the geometry of differences between causal lines under a chosen projection. A metric can be induced as a functional of mismatch across chains, $d(i,j) = \Phi\big(\{x_k^{(i)}\},\{x_k^{(j)}\}\big)$, where $\Phi$ is projection-dependent.

**The Non-Existence of the Now**

There is no ontological present. “Now” is:
- An abstract selection of a virtual reference across congruent relational patterns,
- A coordinate choice within a projection that aligns multiple chains,
- Not a substantive “slice” moving through a time dimension.

This perspective dissolves classical puzzles:
- Measurement: “collapse” is the projective continuation of a composite relation, not a separate dynamical act.
- Entanglement: non-locality is vacuous at the base layer because “space” is already emergent.
- Time’s arrow: temporal asymmetry tracks the direction of projective continuation; thermodynamic irreversibility is the aggregation of information in the interference pattern under chosen coarse-grainings.
- Fractality: infinite resolution explains scale invariance and renormalization behaviors as natural features of projection across scales.

**Conceptual Questions (with Answers)**

- What fixes the “linear relation” of continuation?  
Nothing ontic. There is no medium to define linearity independently. “Linearity” is the organizing effect of projection; relations may be arbitrarily complex underneath yet appear linear relative to the induced order of a chosen projection.

- In what sense is continuation “simultaneous” without time?  
Without a projection there is no ordering, hence no distinction between simultaneous and successive. All relations are equivalently “present”; a projection imposes an order and thereby carves simultaneity and succession.

- How do we differentiate wobbles if nothing substantial exists?  
Differentiation is scale-dependent individuation. A wobble is the equivalence class defined by a chosen resolution; at infinite resolution, boundaries dissolve and every wobble nests sub-wobbles ad infinitum.

- What is the status of “projection”?  
Projection is a mapping that creates both its image and its stage. There is no external substrate; the mapping constitutes the projection surface and the depicted structure in one act.

**Projection Ontology: Radical Consequences**

**The Projection Principle as Primitive**

Thesis: Properties are generated by acts of projection; they are artifacts of mapping, not features of a mind-independent inventory of substances. Observation is interference; description is projection; individuation emerges from imposed distinctions.

**“Linearity” as an Inevitable Artifact**

- Not: Interferences are linear.  
- But: Whatever is registered as interference is organized into linearizable continuations by the projection.

Formally, for an interference operator $\mathcal{I}$ and projection $P$, there typically exists an induced operator $L$ such that $P(\mathcal{I}(w_1, w_2)) = L(P(w_1), P(w_2))$. The “linearity” of $L$ reflects constraints of $P$, not ontic linearity of $\mathcal{I}$.

**Simultaneity Without a Time Medium**

Prior to any ordering, distinctions between “at once” and “one after another” do not exist. A projection introduces:
- Temporal order within a chain (sequential continuation),
- Spatial coexistence across chains (relative displacement).

**Scale Choice as Wobble Definition**

A “wobble” is a scale-bounded cut through a scale-free relational manifold:
- Defined by a resolution operator $S_\sigma$, with $S_\sigma(w)$ the wobble at scale $\sigma$,
- There is no atomic endpoint: $S_{\sigma\to 0}(w)$ unveils endless sub-structure.

**Projection as Self-Referential Mapping**

Projection generates both the image and its surface: $P: \mathcal{R} \to \mathcal{O}$ where “domain” and “codomain” are not prior substances but roles within the mapping. This self-referentiality resembles strange loops and autologous systems but at an ontological base layer.

**Consequences for Physical Concepts**

- Causality: The relation “A causes B” is the order introduced by projection that individuates A and B in the first place.
- Laws of nature: Express invariances of the projection operation. Conservation laws are Noether-like statements about symmetries of $P$ and $S_\sigma$.
- Quantum theory: Indeterminacy reflects incompossibility of incompatible projections. Position and momentum are different scale-perspectives; Heisenberg-type trade-offs arise from geometry of $P$ rather than disturbance.
- Observer: Not privileged; an “observer wobble” is a particular projection that, upon interference, yields a meta-projection interpreted as measurement.

**Arbitrariness vs. Consistency**

Projection seems arbitrary, yet scientific projections are highly stable. Resolution:
- Stable projections are self-reproducing under continuation: $P \circ \mathcal{I} \approx \mathcal{U} \circ P$ for some effective evolution $\mathcal{U}$.
- “Laws” are fixed points (or attractors) of the projection group across scales.
- Scientific success indicates projective self-consistency and wide domains of stability, not access to a substance.

**The Self-Limiting TOE: Paradoxical Epistemology**

**An “Ultimate Utilitarianism”**

If projection is taken as fundamental, theory choice cannot appeal to correspondence with a projection-independent reality. What remains is pragmatic adequacy:
- Maximize predictivity, compressibility, and cross-scale coherence.
- Minimize ontic commitments (Ockham at the limit).
- A projection-centred reading is evaluated by how far it reconstructs observables as projection effects and addresses observability itself within the same vocabulary.

**The Impossibility of Substantial Ontology**

Argument schema:
1) Any ontology individuates entities $\{e_i\}$.  
2) Individuation presupposes distinction.  
3) Distinction is a projective act.  
4) Hence, any ontology is projection-dependent.  
Therefore, no projection-independent substantive ontology is coherent. The only projection-invariant “ontology” would be total undifferentiation—too featureless to count as an ontology in the usual sense.

**A TOE That Denies Its Own Ultimacy**

A consequence of projection ontology is that every theory, the present one included, is itself a projection. The account does not claim to describe “reality as it is”; it describes the invariant structure of projection—constraints that apply to any theorizing conducted in these terms.

Three readings:
- Radical relativism: all TOEs are equally valid nor invalid (nihilistic endpoint).  
- Pragmatic minimalism: the projection reading is preferred for parsimony and breadth of unification within this program.  
- Fixed-point view: the formalism aims to capture invariants under the group of admissible projections, mapping the limits of theorization from inside the projection perspective.

**Practical Consequences**

- Physics: Experiments test projective self-consistency, not mind-independent substances. Rival TOEs (strings, loops, etc.) are different high-stability projections; compare by scope, coherence, and compression.
- AI and consciousness: Consciousness is projection that models itself as a projector; metacognition is nested projection; agency is a stable self-referential fixed point.
- Ethics: Without objective values “out there,” ethics can be grounded in projectional symmetry—policies that respect the legitimacy and coherence of alternative projections.

**The Central Paradox Resolved**

The projection reading does not prove its own reality; the move is instead to read “reality-proofs” as category errors. What is asserted is a restriction on provability, analogous to self-referential limits in formal systems: “No theory is ultimately real” functions as a negative claim about epistemic boundaries, consistently self-applicable.

**Integrated Three-Pillar Approach to ISE**

**I. Architecture of Integration**

- Philosophical foundation
  - Define projection ontology and its commitments.
- Physical framework
  - Instantiate concrete models that reproduce observables as projection-induced invariants across scales.
- Epistemological critique
  - Clarify what experiments and theories actually test and why certain projections stabilize.

These streams interlock:
- Philosophy constrains what counts as an explanation.
- Physics demonstrates constructive adequacy (deriving kinematics/dynamics as projective artifacts).
- Epistemology audits the limits of inference, confirmation, and model equivalence.

**II. Minimal Formal Scaffolding**

- Relational substrate: a scale-free set of patterns $\mathcal{W}$.
- Scale maps: $S_\sigma:\mathcal{W}\to\mathcal{W}$ with self-similarity $S_\lambda(S_\mu(w)) \approx S_{\lambda\mu}(w)$.
- Interference: $\mathcal{I}:\mathcal{W}\times\mathcal{W}\to\mathcal{W}$ (not assumed linear).
- Projection family: $P_{\sigma,\pi}:\mathcal{W}\to\mathcal{O}$ (resolution $\sigma$, perspective $\pi$).
- Induced evolution: $F_{\sigma,\pi}$ such that $P_{\sigma,\pi}(w_{k+1}) = F_{\sigma,\pi}(P_{\sigma,\pi}(w_k))$.
- Laws as invariants: find $\mathcal{G}$ with $P_{\sigma,\pi}\circ \mathcal{I} = \mathcal{U}\circ P_{\sigma,\pi}$ and symmetries of $\mathcal{G}$ yielding conservation via Noether-like correspondences.

**III. Programmatic Consequences and Proto-Predictions**

- Scale covariance of effective laws:
  - Prediction: families of empirical regularities show renormalization-like flows derived from projection changes, not micro-dynamics. Seek universal exponents as projective invariants.
- Relational time reconstruction:
  - Prediction: temporal parameters can be reconstructed from correlation orderings (internal clocks) with gauge-like redundancy; multiple “times” reduce to reparameterizations of $F_{\sigma,\pi}$.
- Nonlocal correlations without signaling:
  - Prediction: violations of Bell-type inequalities persist, while no-signaling holds as a projection symmetry; derive Tsirelson bounds as constraints on $P_{\sigma,\pi}$ geometry.
- Observer relativity without collapse dynamics:
  - Prediction: update rules that look like state-vector reduction emerge as Bayesian-like changes under projection change, aligning with quantum instrument formalism.
- Metric emergence:
  - Prediction: spatial metrics $d$ arise from mismatch functionals over chains; curvature corresponds to nontrivial holonomy of projection changes around loops in $(\sigma,\pi)$-space.

**IV. Positioning Within Philosophy**

The projection reading relates to Kant by dropping the “thing-in-itself” and differs from social constructivism in relocating construction from human agents to projection as a structural operation. It resonates with:
- Relationalism (physics without background structures),
- Process philosophy (becoming over being),
- Information-theoretic approaches (structure without substance),
- Category and topos perspectives (primacy of morphisms over objects).

**V. Limitations and Research Directions**

- Formal completeness: Specify admissible projection families and their symmetry groups.
- Equivalence criteria: Develop rigorous tests for when two projections are empirically indistinguishable yet conceptually distinct.
- Measurement theory: Unify operational quantum frameworks (POVMs, instruments) as projective transformations.
- Geometry from projection: Derive effective spacetime structures (Lorentzian signature, causal cones) as stability conditions of $P_{\sigma,\pi}$.
- Thermodynamics: Ground entropy and irreversibility in projective compression maps and their monotone functionals $H$ with $H(F(x)) \ge H(x)$.

**Closing Perspective**

ISE reframes time, causality, and space as artifacts of projection operating on a scale-free relational manifold. Causal order is the projective continuation of interference, time is the induced sequencing, space is the structure of cross-chain differences, and the “present” is an abstract alignment of congruent patterns. Laws are invariants of projection; measurement is interference-as-update; and stability, not substance, explains why physics is effective. As a self-limiting TOE, ISE denies ontic ultimacy while offering a unifying meta-structure that organizes explanation, prediction, and method across scales.
