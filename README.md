# Infinite Scale Expansion (ISE)
Exploring Scale-Free Quantum Fields

Author: M.Sc. Physics Gordon Anatta Shaamvai and Colleagues  
Institute of Theoretical Physics, University of Unalaska  
License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Overview
- ISE reframes reality from entities to relations: phenomena emerge from resonant differentials via continuous scalar differentiation.
- Space, time, mass, information are not pre-given; they emerge as projections along resonant axes.
- Observation is resonant participation; there is no external validation frame. Reality is scale-relative.

Core Shift
- Ontology: from substance to differentiation. Classical ontology is dissolved into resonance.
- ISE is a perspective shift, not a conventional theory: no fixed foundations, only dynamic, scale-dependent structures.

Key Mechanisms
- Scalar Synchronized Differentiation: energy differentiates coherently across all scales; cross-scale coupling yields structure without singularities or disconnected forces.
- Dynamic Relation of Scales: scales co-evolve; micro-macro influences are bidirectional, with no privileged scale.

Key Concepts
- Infinite Expansion and Differentiation: continuous refining of energy generates emergent dimensions and interactions; spacetime is a byproduct.
- Protoinformation and Quantum Fields: protoinformation precedes spacetime; quantum fields extend it, linking micro to macro emergence.
- Cosmological Consequences: dark energy as emergent differentiation; no singular Big Bang—an ongoing unfolding replaces initial singularities.
- Fractality and Multiverse: self-similar differentiation across scales; universes as differentiated states within an infinite hierarchy.
- Emergent Spacetime and Causality: causality is scale-dependent and relational; time flow varies with differentiation level.
- No Singularities: apparent singularities are intense differentiation zones, not infinities.
- Quantum-to-Macro Continuity: quantum effects scale into macroscopic structure; the quantum/classical divide is a matter of scale.

Resonance Framework
- Universe as Resonance Zone: stable resonance layers within a dimensionless quantum field define perceived reality.
- Lower Boundary (Quantum): superposition and uncertainty arise from diminishing resonance at small scales.
- Upper Boundary (High Energy): black holes/Planck regime mark resonance transitions to deeper scale orders; horizons are transitional zones.
- Black Holes and Big Bang: gateways/cascades of resonance reorganization; universes form as scale-localized resonance phenomena.

Implications
- Unified lens for quantum mechanics, cosmology, and high-energy physics via scale-expansive resonance.
- Eliminates need for external absolutes and singular origins; emphasizes coherent differentiation across scales.

Scope
- Conceptual framework prioritizing theoretical implications over formal mathematics; aims to reorient foundational assumptions about time, space, causality, and ontology.
