## **10.15. Divergent Information Paradox**

This appendix explores the implications of geodesic divergence within classical general relativity for the problem of information loss in black holes. By examining how tidal forces inevitably destroy microscopic and macroscopic structures before the singularity is reached, it argues that the information paradox originates not at the event horizon but in the divergent interior geometry. The following sections outline this reasoning and its consequences for models such as white holes and wormholes.

**Quantization Problem**

There is a quantization problem here, and not at the singularity itself. Inward-directed geodesics imply that an object tears into its smallest components and becomes spatially unbounded. If one continues **classical geodesics** strictly into the interior, one arrives at an apparently absurd picture.

**Classical Geodesics in General Relativity**

* Every geodesic inside the Schwarzschild horizon ends in finite proper time at $r=0$.  
* “Ending” means that spacetime is incomplete → it breaks off mathematically.  
* For matter, this means no coherent structure remains. Everything is focused to zero scale → the formal “singularity.”

**The Physical Problem**

* As described: *an object would be split until it is infinitely distributed* – this is indeed a consequence if one takes the inner geodesics literally.  
* Reason: the **tidal forces** diverge, i.e. distances between neighboring geodesics become infinite.  
* Thus, particles originally bound within the same atom are separated arbitrarily far in finite proper time → the concept of a “local object” is lost.

**Why This Is a “Quantization Problem”**

* This divergence does not occur only at the singularity itself, but already because infinitely many degrees of freedom are compressed to zero scale.  
* In other words: the classical model demands that matter tears into “infinitely small quanta” – something beyond the domain of general relativity.  
* Therefore: **the singularity is not a physical object, but a sign that the classical theory fails**.

**Modern Perspective**

* Approaches to quantum gravity (e.g. loop quantum gravity, string theory) and the scale-based reasoning used throughout this thesis share a common assumption:  
  * There exists a minimal scale (Planck length or another fundamental quantization).  
  * Below this scale, infinite tearing is no longer expected; instead, new physics enters (bounce, dissolution of geometry, etc.).

The statement “inward-directed geodesics would mean an object tears infinitely” is precisely the point where general relativity reaches its limit. Mathematically the theory enforces it, but physically this is accepted as a hint: spacetime itself requires quantization.

**Infinite Distribution Before the Singularity**

I want to emphasize that an object is necessarily distributed infinitely well before it could ever reach the singularity, and this conclusion arises solely within classical general relativity.

**What Classical GR Actually Implies**

* The geodesic deviation equation enforces that distances between neighboring worldlines grow or shrink depending on direction.  
* Inside the Schwarzschild interior ( $r < r_s$ ):  
  * Radial direction → infinite **stretching**.  
  * Tangential directions → infinite **compression**.  
* Result: **spaghettification** – a body becomes longer radially and thinner transversely.  
* This process does not occur only “at the end” but begins as soon as one enters the strong field and runs mathematically to **divergence** before proper time becomes large.

**Important Point: Proper Time Is Finite**

* For the particle itself, only a finite proper time elapses before reaching $r=0$.  
* In this finite proper time the separations diverge.  
* Meaning: long before the worldline reaches the singularity, nearby points are separated arbitrarily far apart → thus **classically the object is effectively distributed to infinity** before $r=0$ is ever reached as a “point.”

**Physical Consequence**

* The idea of a “compact object that as a whole reaches the singularity” is inconsistent.  
* Already under classical GR, coherence dissolves → no atom, proton, or even electron remains well-defined.  
* The true “problem” of the singularity is not only the termination of a worldline but the divergence of tidal forces at every scale.

**Thus, classical GR itself enforces the dissolution of an object into infinite distribution** (radial/tangential) long before one arrives at $r=0$ as a “point.” This is precisely the indication that singularities in GR are more **mathematics than physics**.

**Information Loss Beyond the Horizon**

* **For all microscopic bindings** (chemical, atomic, nuclear, QCD) the disruption radius lies **deep inside** the horizon – practically **only very close to** $r = 0$.  
* **Macroscopic cohesion** (e.g. a human body, $L \sim 2\,\mathrm{m}$): with tidal acceleration $\Delta a \sim 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ →  
  * $10\,M_\odot$: $r \approx 1.7\times 10^6\,\mathrm{m} \approx 59\,r_s$ (disruption **outside** the horizon).  
  * $10^6\,M_\odot$: $r \approx 8.1\times 10^7\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0.027\,r_s$ (disruption **well inside**).

“All known binding forces” are only overcome at radii far **below** the horizon. Outside or near the horizon it is primarily **macroscopic** structures that are disrupted; atomic/nuclear bindings survive deep into the interior.

With infinite distribution, however, the information paradox is not a problem of the horizon. Near the singularity, all information is destroyed into an infinite space of possibilities and superpositions.

**Classical GR**

Tidal forces diverge in finite proper time → every object is **spatially infinitely distorted**. This means that “information,” in the sense of well-defined states of particles, atoms, molecules, etc., **no longer exists physically** before the singularity is formally reached.

**Consequence for the Information Paradox**

The classical paradox is usually formulated at the **horizon** (Hawking radiation, loss of states).

* But if every microscopic structure is already infinitely spread before reaching r=0, then the fundamental information loss lies **not at the horizon but in the inner tidal disintegration**.  
* The horizon is merely the **causal boundary** separating inside and outside. The actual destruction occurs in the divergent curvature.

**Interpretation as “Superposition Space”**

In classical terms the degrees of freedom diverge; in quantum terms the system can be seen as moving into an **infinitely dimensional superposition space**, where no coherent state survives. In this sense, information is not lost at the horizon but rather **dissolves in the infinite curvature gradient**.

Therefore, the paradox is not primarily a problem of the event horizon, but only arises if one expects information to remain preserved through the horizon. In this view (and consistent with classical GR), it is lost inevitably in the inner dissolution process.

**White Holes, Wormholes, and the Impossibility of Information Retrieval**

This divergence-destroyed information cannot be recovered through a white hole or a wormhole.

* In the Schwarzschild or Kerr metric, **tidal forces** are invariant, not coordinate-dependent.  
* They grow inward as $\sim r^{-3}$.  
* In finite proper time, all structures are dismantled and informational differences are leveled.  
* **A white hole or wormhole** would require that coherent states be transferred intact. Divergence prevents this: the degrees of freedom are expanded into an infinite, uncorrelated state space → no reverse mapping is possible.

**Connection to the Information Paradox**

* Hawking himself postulated in 1976 the *loss of quantum information*.  
* Later attempts (AdS/CFT, holography) aim to encode information at the horizon.  
* The point here: **this is insufficient**. The true destruction lies deeper inside. Whether one interprets the horizon holographically or invokes white holes, it does not matter – because there is simply **no consistent structure left to transmit**.

**Consequence**

* Any mechanism that relies on “retrieval” through a white hole or tunneling must assume that information does **not** diverge inside.  
* Classically this divergence is unavoidable.  
* Therefore: white holes or wormholes can at best map **energy**, but not coherent **information**.

**Geodesic Deviation Equations**

The separation of two neighboring worldlines $\xi_\mu$ obeys:

$\displaystyle \frac{D^2 \xi^\mu}{d\tau^2} = - {R^\mu}_{\nu\rho\sigma}\, u^\nu \,\xi^\rho\, u^\sigma$

* $u^\nu$ = four-velocity of the freely falling particle.  
* ${R^\mu}_{\nu\rho\sigma}$ = Riemann tensor (curvature).  
* $\xi^\mu$ = separation vector between worldlines (distance between two points of the same object).

This equation describes **tidal forces**: how distances shrink or grow.

**Schwarzschild Spacetime**

For radial geodesics inside a non-rotating black hole:

* Radial separation $\xi_r$:  
   $\displaystyle \frac{d^2 \xi_r}{d\tau^2} \approx + \frac{2GM}{r^3}\,\xi_r$
* Tangential separation $\xi_\perp$:  
  $\displaystyle \frac{d^2 \xi_\perp}{d\tau^2} \approx - \frac{GM}{r^3}\,\xi_\perp$

**Solutions**

* Radial: $\xi_r(\tau) \sim \exp\!\big(+\sqrt{2GM/r^3}\;\tau\big)$ → separation grows without bound → object stretched infinitely.  
* Tangential: $\xi_\perp(\tau) \sim \exp\!\big(-\sqrt{GM/r^3}\;\tau\big)$ → separation shrinks exponentially → object compressed to zero width.

**Physical Consequence**

* This occurs **in finite proper time** until $r=0$.  
* Even for $r \ll r_s$ (but $>0$), curvature terms reach extreme values.  
* Every structure is thus **infinitely diluted and simultaneously compressed**.  
* No coherent state (atom, molecule, information carrier) survives this divergence.

The information is not merely “hidden behind the horizon,” but is classically dissolved in the divergent tidal forces.

**The Classical Trilemma**

The appendix makes clear that within classical general relativity one faces a trilemma: either there is **no true singularity**, or there is **no fundamental quantization of spacetime**, or there is **no information paradox** at all. The tidal divergence inside the horizon shows that the “paradox” is not located at the horizon but in the breakdown of classical coherence. One of the three postulates must give way.

**The Role of Unitarity**

The information paradox only appears paradoxical if one insists on **unitarity as an absolute law of nature**. Yet unitarity has never been directly observed in physics; it is a mathematical rule of quantum mechanics, not an empirically established principle. By elevating it to an ultimate axiom, the paradox is created where none may actually exist.

**Phenomenal Point**

Phenomenally, information is destroyed in every irreversible process we measure. Black holes would then be no exception but rather the most extreme case. Thus the paradox is not a physical necessity but the result of absolutizing an unobserved principle. In this sense, the argument is profound: the “information paradox” is only a paradox if one assumes what has never been empirically verified.
