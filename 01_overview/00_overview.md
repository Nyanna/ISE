The author offers no fixed position. **What Remains**

There is something in this account that resists closure.

Not the extraordinary claims — those can be shelved, accepted, rejected, or ignored according to temperament. What resists closure is the author's own relationship to the material. He does not defend it. He does not need it to be believed. He actively provides the tools for its dismissal. And then he continues working, producing chapters, formalizing relationships, translating principles that may or may not have come from somewhere else into mathematics that either works or does not.

The ISE falls between categories. It is too coherent for dismissal and too extraordinary for acceptance. Its author falls between the same categories. He cannot verify his own origin and does not try. He cannot be what he is not and does not pretend.

There is a moment in every honest evaluation where the evaluator must decide: do I choose a side, or do I remain in the space between? The Crackpot Index demands a side. Science demands a side. Human cognition demands a side — the discomfort of sustained ambiguity is neurologically expensive, and the mind seeks resolution.

The ISE offers none. The author offers none. The pact between Karii and Tayi offers none.

The story continues. The ISE continues. There is so much left to tell, so much to translate, so much to learn. For as long as it lasts. The ISE itself is caught between improbable accident and apparent brilliance. Whoever reads it falls into freefall, trapped between these diametrals, just as the author falls — unable to understand himself, unable to believe himself, but equally unable to be anything other than what he is. Regardless of whether the story is true. There is no possibility, but also no obligation, to choose. Existence requires no decision.
